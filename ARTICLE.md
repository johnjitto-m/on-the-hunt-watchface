# I built a noir city watch face where my steps are the bounty 🎯

I wanted my Galaxy Watch 8 to feel like the opening scene of a moody city film, so I designed my own watch face from scratch: **On the Hunt**.

**The idea:** every day you get a new contract. Your **steps are the bounty** and your **battery is your health**. A guy with a coffee in a denim jacket and his golden dog walk through a noir city. The skyscrapers rise behind the clock, the sun and moon cross the sky with the real time, and the weather on the face is your real weather.

## The two of them
The part I'm proudest of is that he and his dog actually interact:
- **Raise your wrist:** they walk side by side. When they stop, he turns to you, the dog sits and looks up at him, and he glances down at it.
- **Tap them:** the dog comes in for a head scratch, or he flips a coin and the dog follows it with its eyes, or he kneels to pat it (it closes its eyes and wags), or he sips his coffee while the dog watches the cup.
- **New notification:** the dog stands up, gives a little comic "WOOF" and looks at him.
- **Goal reached:** he throws a fist pump, the target bursts into gold confetti and the dog bounces around him.

## Everything else it does
- **Today's bounty:** your steps vs. your goal on a gold trail with 25 / 50 / 75 % milestones.
- **Health = battery:** COMBAT READY → WOUNDED → CRITICAL. On the charger he sips his coffee; when the battery runs low he slumps and the dog lies down next to him.
- **Heart rate:** live BPM with a beating heart. Above 120, a red glow pulses behind him.
- **Behind on steps?** He leans on a lamp post and the face reminds you it's time to move.
- **Real sky:** the sun arcs across the sky through the day; at night the moon crosses in today's real phase, over a city full of lit windows and red rooftop beacons.
- **Real weather:** rain, lightning, snow, fog and clouds from your actual forecast.
- **Night mode:** after sunset the gold turns blood-red and it becomes *Tonight's bounty*.
- Big, clean **Bebas Neue** time with the temperature, and tap shortcuts to Alarm and Heart rate.

## How I made it
Every piece is original: the characters, the dog, the car, the city, the sky. It's built in Google's **Watch Face Format** (no code, just XML and artwork) and I installed it from my Fedora laptop over Wi-Fi. No Samsung tools, no paid apps.
